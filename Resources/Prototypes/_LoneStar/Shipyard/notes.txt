- Folder Notations
Civillian:
Any ship purchased through Nash or other neutral shipyards.

Freelancer:
Any ships access locked to Freelancers

Lsrd:
Any ships access locked to the LSRD

Scrap:
Any ship sold at the scrap shipyard console

Expedition:
Any ship capable of expeditions, this is a priority folder.
If a ship pertains to another group but can exped, it goes in Expedition.

Mercenary:
Any ship designed for combat, or expected to be damaged in possible ship-to-ship combat.
These should be cheap, and come ready to fly out.
Should not be outfitted with loose equipment such as weapons, armor, or medical supply.

Worker:
Any ship purposed for job-related work, mining/service/cargo etc.
Should have a starting supply to get to work on the job it sets out to let you do and nothing more.

Other:
Ships that are on the chopping block, or are looking to be deprecated.

- Pricing and mapping notes
Ships should be priced at round values such as 16,250 or 85,000 675,000.

Use best judgement on what size a ship falls under, the following are examples and not rules.
Micro: Salto / Construct
Small: Jabber / Prospector / Camper
Medium: Whelp / Pathfinder / Brigand
Large: Fortress / Charon / Petrel
Titan: Yegerov / Ambition / Anchor

Piping and atmos
- Aim to keep all distro pipes on the central 'primary' pipe layer.
- Aim to keep all waste pipes on the top-right 'secondary' pipe layer.
- Aim to give all rooms one scrubber and air vent, though small non-bedroom 'closets' and such, are not required to have vents.
- Outside of atmos/gas-mining ships, do not add weird or avantgarde piping setups. the average shuttle is never modified, and when it is, these only serve to complicate peoples plans.
- All relevant atmos pipes must use the default 'distro' and 'waste' spray painter colours
- Use O2 + N2 distro inputs if space provides, lee-way is given to small and micro ships to use air-mix or respiron canisters.

Electrical Grids
- Keep all wires from going under walls, if a thruster cant be powered without doing so, explore moving the thruster first.
- Ships should have atleast some reserve fuel, ideally in a wall-closet
- All expedition ships must be powered by an AME.
- Radioactive generators (bananium) must include a radiation suit closet and shutters linked to buttons.
- Radioactive generators may not come premapped with hard-shielding, such as uranium window panes placed overtop the generator.
- Radioactive generators must also not be mapped so that radiation leaks its confinement, especially into the exterior of the ship.
- A ship must be able to power itself with its default configuration.
- Any SMESs should not feed power back into themselves and should be on a HV wire isolated from the generation source.
- Any SMESs should feed their power into substation.
- If a shuttle has 2 SMES and still uses a wallmount substation, replace 1 for a regular substation instead.
- Do not mess with default SMES/Substation settings.
- LV Grids should be neat and concise, and not plastering the entire floor space.
- LV Grids should aim to ensure all interior tiles of the ship are covered with power.
- Ships should not use a single APC to power their entirety outside of the sensibilities of a micro ship.

Blastdoors & Shutters
- Blast doors leading to externals should be shut by default
- Blast doors under windows should be open by default.
- Other interior-interior doors may be in either starting states as you see relevant.
- All blastdoors on windows should have atleast 1 nearby framed button (see below) in the room they are attached to.

Button frame standards
- Red for 'lockdown' or 'panic' functions
- Yellow for thruster toggles or engineering related systems
- Purple for reclaimer/disposals systems
- Colorless for window shutters/interior doors
- EXIT for any blast doors leading to exteriors

Air alarms & Firelocks
- Shuttles must have atleast 1 air alarm somewhere
- Air alarms must be placed on interiors of the ship with exceptions to atmos/gas-miner shuttles.
- Not every room needs an air alarm, however all devices should be linked to an air alarm.
- Firelocks must be beneath all interior airlocks and never under external-facing airlocks.
- Use the craftable 'Directional fans' and not 'Tiny fans' to keep pressure on ship edges.
- When using decorative 'stairs', ensure firelocks are not placed at the 'lower' end of the stairs, this also applies to airlocks.

Lighting
- AlwaysPowered lights may only be used for debug purposes.
- Hardened lights may only be used on POIs in command areas, or for POIs with very little visiting purpose such as the court house.
- Shielded lights should only be mapped on shuttle exteriors.
- Regular light fixtures should be used in all situations outside of the prior mentioned cases.
- Use red powered lights on the left-lower-exterior of the ship, and green on the right-lower-exterior of the ship.
- Use atleast a single exterior light on the north or south end of the ship, any extra exterior lights must be exterior.
- Post-lights must be put behind railings or on navigable external lattice
- It is recommended to put a strobe light near any blast doors that lead to space, linked to on/off/toggles of the relevant blast doors.
- Mapped Strobe lights must be linked to a button for toggling.
- Shuttles must contain some degree of emergency lights.

Thrusters
- Use Mules only, some shuttles may have dragons or other strange configurations, however these are exceptions and planned to be phased out for alternatives in the future.
- Ensure no thrusters are toggled off by accident while mapping, you can inspect on thrusters to check it says "on"
- Due to bugs with mapping initialization, all thrusters must be on even if a bi-modal state is supported via buttons.
- Thrusters should always point into empty void space which are connected to exterior bounds of the Shuttles.
- Thrusters should ideally be placed on lattice with catwalks under.

Loot and providings
- Ships may start with a basic materials crate (30 steel/plastic/glass), this should be the entirety of the ships provided material.
- Avoid giving extrenuous supplies to shuttles that would not otherwise need it, such as topicals to a salvage ship, 'because they get hurt often' is not a reason.
- Outside of 'resturants' or 'intermediate area' shuttles such as the crossroads, avoid giving shuttles any form of vending machines.
- BioDjinns may only be mapped on shuttles that are solely presenting as resturant or botany shuttles.
- Techfabs such as engi/merc/salv/med/cargo/service are only to be mapped if the ships intentions are to be used for those domains, salv fab on a mining ship, med fab on a medical ship.
- Consider replacing techfabs with autolathes if no domains are present, or providing a cargo techfab.
- Ships should start with atleast 1 EVA relevant to a domain it exists under, e.g. FSB medical eva for parmedical ships.
- Only mercenary domain ships may start with 'mercenary' or equivelant hardsuits.

Decals
- Always use pre-assinged palette colors when mapping.
- Priorize using frontier/departmental or other color palettes that feature translucent palette colors as this will give softer results.
- Do not deviate from palette colors solely because "i want it bright and garish", thats not a style choice, it will sore whoever is buying the ship fast.
- With 'brick' markings using color, ensure they are placed on existing brick markings without color.
- Decals are art, and can be hard to do right. ask about and share your progress if you are unsure or need help.

Walls and type rules
- Standard shuttles should be mapped with either the wall/reinforced wall set, or shuttle wall set.
- Combat focused factional shuttles should be mapped with either the shuttle wall set or, plastitanium wall set.
- Only freelancer ships may be mapped with the mining wall set.
- Only freelancer/lsrd ships may be mapped with the plastitanium wall set.
- freelancer ships may not be mapped with the shuttle wall set
- Airlocks and general access on factional combat ships must be properly set with locked door variants. (pirate for Freelancers, security for LSRD)
- Shutters (opposed to blast doors) should be used on non factional ships for windows

Other Standard requirements
- Station Records computer
- Latejoin spawning marker (this is for interview applicants)
- Ghost warp point

Ship shapes and shaped lattices
- Avoid the use of diagonal walls on interiors (due to hitboxes they are incredibly awkward to interact around)
- Crimp exterior lattices into diagonal platings on exteriors of the ship.
- Avoid the use of unessecary lattices on the exterior of the ship for decorative reasons.
- Avoid making ships that are entirely left/right symmetrical in floorplan.
- IFF shapes of ships may be symmetrical, however avoid doing so solely for the sake of symmetry.
- Cockpits and shuttle consoles must always be placed facing the correct direction.
- Ships must always be build with default north/up being forward.
- There must always be atleast 1 docking airlock.

What defines a ship
- Medical;
  - Typically comes with a triage/ward
  - Anything outside of a ship entirely aimed at paramedical should feature a cryotube.
  - Chem area though not required to be stocked
  - Ideally has atleast a couple botany trays.
- Engineering;
  - Gas miners or typically an extension onto salavge style mining ships
  - Use reservation when considering a ship under 'engineering' if it is not doing gas mining.
- Mercenary;
  - Typically exped capable, otherwise likely a combat focused ship. (will use pirate/NFSD fabs in future, but for now use mercenary)
  - Low amounts of livery, typically comes with room space to install machines.
  - BangWiz and silos, they typically get messy, which we need to prevent via mapping the things they need.
- Salvage;
  - Ideally Non exped, salvage ships should focus more on mining and vroids.
  - Mining supplies, salvage catch-bays
  - Refiner set-ups and belts premade.
- Service;
  - Any ship that is primarily cooking food
  - May be offering cleaning
  - Or doing non medical focused botany.
- Cargo;
  - Cargo crate racks, ensure it also comes with an EMU.
- Science;
  - Has atleast one artifact research chamber.
  - May have an anomaly generator (only for medium and up. Size of ship = easier to handle and less abusable.).
  - Protolathe Autolathe & Lithograph as a baseline.
- Combat Ships;
  - Should feature some basic level 'panic' button to lock down its primary doors.
  - All secure areas should feature blastdoors for windows.
  - Suggested to feature a holding cell if size permits, however not required.
- Factional Exped ships;
  - Must have a BangWiz, along with 1 gunrack.
  - Should ideally have some form of combat viability. this does not mean cannons, this means secondary exits and personnel-safety.
- Exped Ships;
  - Must have a reclaimer and silo, and be powered by AME.
- All Ships
  - All ships may have livery areas and area recommended to have atleast 1 bedroom (even if there is a communal barracks).
  - 'Captain bedrooms' should feature a captain-locked airlock, and ideally a captain-locked safe.
  - As ship size increases its recommened to add lounge space or a barracks for players to AFK/Log.
  - May have a bar, but not areas to cook food such as grills or ranges.
  - Should utilize its wall space as best it can for closets/posters/safety-devices
  - Must feature a defibrillator 'somewhere' even if not in a cabinet, and it must be accessible without vaulting tables.

Editor related tips & tricks:

the `colornetwork` command can be used to quickly color your pipe network.
use `colornetwork [uid] Pipe #ffffff` to do so, waste red is #990000 and distro blue is #0055cc

shuttles must be saved as grids, you can run `savegrid [grid ID] /[name].yml`.
to get the grid ID, press f3 and look for the grid ID field in the blue block of debug information while you're on the grid you've created
you can find grids you have saved in `/bin/Content.Server/data/`, which you can then copy and paste into /Resources/Maps/_LoneStar/Shuttles/

you can run `appraisegrid [grid ID]` to get the current appraised value of the ship for pricing ideas.
note that this doesnt count things that will spawn in later, as while mapping, 'filled' closets and other items are empty.

when searching in the f5 menu for entities, you can search by either the raw name of the entity, or the prototype ID
prototype ID can sometimes be more useful for similar items, you can see the prototype ID of an entity by right clicking it and seeing it in bracks `small light(SmallLight)`

Useful hotkeys
f5 - Entity spawn menu
f6 - Tile menu
f8 - Decal menu (not identical to spray painters, it lacks the pipette feature, but has more decal options)

if its your first time mapping after you have set up a local environment heres a quick guide on how to start;
run `mapping 55555 /Maps/_LoneStar/Shuttles/Civillian/Worker/jabber.yml false`
this should load up the jabber ship into a new map.
55555 can be any 5 or 6 digit number, this is just to ensure that the map you start editing on isnt in use, i usually spam some random number every time.

not comfortable being able to use github? ask in #dev-general or #mapping after you've made a .yml file and someone will likely be able to upload it on your behalf.
